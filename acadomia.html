Who We Are

Thirty-five years of educational excellence

Acadomia was founded in 1989 with a singular conviction: that every person deserves access to personalized, expert instruction. In the decades since, it has grown into France's most trusted name in private education — a group whose reach extends from the primary school pupil to the adult learner, from Strasbourg to Nice, from Paris to the world.

Headquartered in the 8th arrondissement of Paris — the heart of the city's educational and professional life — Acadomia operates at the intersection of academic rigour and genuine human connection. Its pedagogy is not transactional. It is relational, iterative, and deeply committed to the progress of each individual student.

From the Loire Valley to the boulevards of Paris, Acadomia's teachers are rooted in the life of France.

The Acadomia Philosophy

Personalized learning at every level

Acadomia's founding insight was simple but radical: the best way to learn is one-to-one, or in very small groups, with an expert who truly understands your level, your pace, and your goals. This philosophy — of personalized instruction built around the individual — runs through everything Acadomia does, from its tutoring programs to The French Atelier's intimate live classes of just 8–10 learners.

When you study with The French Atelier, you inherit 35 years of pedagogical experience. The methods, the standards, the care for student progress — all of it flows from Acadomia's long tradition of excellence in French education.

A French Institution

Rooted in France.
Present everywhere.

Acadomia's community spans every corner of France — from Lille to Marseille, from the Atlantic coast to the Alsatian border. Its teachers, tutors, and students share a single conviction: that education, done properly, changes a life.

Institutional Partnerships

Trusted by France's finest

Acadomia's standing in French life is affirmed by the calibre of the institutions that have chosen to partner with it.

Official Partnership

Paris Saint-Germain

Acadomia is an official educational partner of Paris Saint-Germain, the capital's legendary football club. The partnership reflects a shared belief in ambition, discipline, and the pursuit of excellence — values that animate both elite sport and the finest education.

Official Partnership

French Tennis Federation

Acadomia's partnership with the Fédération Française de Tennis underscores its place at the heart of French sporting and cultural life. Precision, perseverance, and a passion for improvement are values shared by every great tennis player — and every great student.

Annual Distinction

Le Trophée
des Passionnés

Each year, Acadomia celebrates the teachers, tutors, and learners who embody its spirit of passion and dedication through the Trophée des Passionnés — its annual award ceremony honouring those who have gone beyond the ordinary in the pursuit of knowledge.

In 2025, the Trophée drew more than 300 candidates — a testament to the depth of talent and commitment within the Acadomia community. The award is a reminder that education, at its best, is not a transaction. It is a vocation.

The Connection

Why Acadomia built
The French Atelier

For decades, Acadomia has helped learners in France master the subjects they love. The French Atelier is the natural extension of that mission to the world — bringing the same personalized, expert, culturally-rooted approach to French language instruction for adults everywhere who wish to truly speak French. Not grammar-French. Not textbook-French. Living, breathing, contextual French.

When you enroll in The French Atelier, you do not simply sign up for a language course. You join a tradition — a community of learners and teachers united by a love of France and the conviction that language is inseparable from culture.
