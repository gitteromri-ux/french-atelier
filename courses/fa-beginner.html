What you'll learn

By unit 20, you will…

Beginner is the level where French stops being phrases and starts being conversation. Here is what A1.2 unlocks.

  • Tell stories in the past with the passé composé (avoir & être)
  • Make plans and predictions with the near future (futur proche)
  • Describe places, people and experiences with richer adjectives
  • Navigate travel: stations, hotels, directions and asking for help
  • Hold a five-minute conversation about your week without switching to English
  • Understand the gist of short announcements, menus and signs

The full curriculum

Twenty units, one journey home to Paris

A structured A1.1→A1.2 curriculum, each 85-minute live unit themed to the road from Normandy to the capital.

01
Welcome to Normandy

Bienvenue en Normandie

Re-activate your A1.1 French and meet the group. Greetings, introductions and a warm-up conversation.

02
At the station

À la gare

Buy train tickets, read a timetable and ask about platforms. Future plans with aller + infinitive.

03
The seaside hotel

L’hôtel au bord de mer

Check in, describe a room and report a problem. Polite requests and the verb pouvoir.

04
The Normandy market

Le marché normand

Shop for regional produce: cheese, cider, seafood. Quantities, weights and comparing prices.

05
Yesterday I…

Hier, j’ai…

First steps into the past: the passé composé with avoir for regular verbs. Recount your day.

06
A walk in Étretat

Une promenade à Étretat

Describe a place and an outing. Adjectives of size and beauty, and prepositions of movement.

07
The past with être

Le passé avec être

Movement verbs in the passé composé (aller, venir, partir) and agreement of the participle.

08
At the doctor’s

Chez le médecin

Describe symptoms and understand simple advice. Body parts and avoir mal à.

09
Inviting a friend

Inviter un ami

Suggest an outing, accept or decline politely, and fix the details. On + suggestions.

10
Regional cooking

La cuisine de région

Follow and describe a simple recipe. Sequencing words: d’abord, ensuite, enfin.

11
Recounting the weekend

Raconter le week-end

Tell a connected story about your weekend, mixing avoir and être in the past.

12
Getting around

Les transports

Compare ways of travelling and explain a route. Comparatives plus/moins/aussi… que.

13
Describing people

Décrire les gens

Describe character and appearance in detail. Adjective agreement and position.

14
At the post office

Au bureau de poste

Send a parcel, buy stamps and fill in a simple form. Indirect object pronouns lui/leur.

15
Travel plans

Projets de voyage

Plan a trip in detail using the futur proche. Express intentions and hopes.

16
Back towards Paris

Retour vers Paris

Talk through a journey from start to finish, combining past and future in one story.

17
A café in Saint-Germain

Un café à Saint-Germain

Re-enter Parisian life: catch up with a friend and share news. Question words in conversation.

18
My tastes in detail

Mes goûts en détail

Give opinions and reasons. Parce que, donc, and richer expressions of like and dislike.

19
Writing a message

Écrire un message

Write a friendly email or text recounting your trip. Linking words and basic paragraphing.

20
The big conversation

La grande conversation

A live role-play weaving the whole journey together. You finish a confident A1.2.
