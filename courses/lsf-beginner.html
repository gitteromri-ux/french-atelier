The full syllabus

10 lessons. One journey.

LSF Beginner is the spoken companion to FA Beginner: a live small-group speaking room where the practical situations of daily life in France — shopping, getting around, eating out, describing your home and routine — become spoken reflexes rather than written rules.

01
Faire les courses à voix haute

In the speaking room you do your shopping out loud — asking for quantities, prices and produce — and react to the vendor. The can-do: complete a spoken shopping exchange.

Ordering & paying role-play
02
Se déplacer en ville

You ask for and follow directions aloud, buy a ticket, and check you're on the right route. The outcome: navigate a French town by speaking.

Live small-group speaking room
03
Commander et payer au restaurant

You run a full restaurant role-play — ordering courses, asking about dishes, paying — entirely by voice. You can now manage a spoken meal from start to finish.

Ordering & paying role-play
04
Décrire son logement

You describe your home out loud — rooms, furniture, what you like about it — and ask others about theirs. The can-do: give a spoken description of where you live.

Describing your day
05
Parler de sa routine

You talk through your daily routine aloud, sequencing actions with d'abord, ensuite, après. You leave able to recount a typical day in speech.

Describing your day
06
Exprimer ses préférences

You say what you like and dislike out loud and justify it simply, comparing tastes with a partner. The outcome: state and defend a preference in conversation.

Live small-group speaking room
07
Prendre rendez-vous

You arrange an appointment by voice — proposing times, negotiating, confirming — as in a real phone call. You can now fix a meeting out loud.

Ordering & paying role-play
08
Réagir à l'imprévu

You handle spoken surprises — a closed shop, a wrong order — improvising an alternative without a script. The can-do: respond to the unexpected on the spot.

Live small-group speaking room
09
Raconter sa journée

You narrate your day in the past tense aloud, chaining events and reactions in connected speech. You leave able to tell a short spoken story of what you did.

Describing your day
10
Bilan parlé — la vie quotidienne

Your final session weaves shopping, travel, dining and description into one extended spoken exchange — proof you can now handle daily French life by voice, scriptless.

Questions & answers

Everything you need to know

Including how it works, the certificate, and what it costs.

LSF Beginner is built for learners at CEFR A1.1 entering at A1.1 and finishing around A1.2. It assumes you've reached the level just below and want to consolidate and grow. Lessons are live, in small groups, and led by native certified teachers broadcasting from France.

You attend 10 live 60 min sessions in groups of 6–8 learners. Every session is a live small-group speaking room — you spend the hour talking, not reading, building spoken reflexes from the first minute. Recordings are available so you never miss a step.

Yes. On completion you receive a CEFR-aligned certificate of achievement issued by Acadomia, France's #1 private learning institute. Our framework follows the same Common European levels recognised across French academia and employers — the standard of reference used by institutions including the Sorbonne.

The French Atelier is presented by Acadomia — founded in 1989 and France's #1 private learning institute — in partnership with the eTeacher award-winning live online learning platform. You learn from native, certified French teachers, supported around the clock by Juliane, our 24/7 AI tutor.

Absolutely. An education advisor will help you place into the right course and build a plan around your schedule and goals.

LSF Beginner is a 10-session speaking course offered from $42 / week on the annual plan, with flexible monthly options. Your advisor will tailor a plan to your goals. Pricing is always discussed with your advisor so it fits your situation — there are no hidden fees, and a $100 learning credit is available on completion.
