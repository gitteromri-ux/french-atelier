Course Overview

Speaking French from lesson one

Designed for complete beginners attending evening sessions. LSF Foundation runs parallel to FA Foundation and covers the same entry level, with a focus on high-frequency survival situations and confidence-building. By the end of this module, you can introduce yourself, count, name objects, describe in simple terms, order a drink, and hold a very short closing conversation.

LSF is the evening speaking track of the French Atelier. It is designed for adults who want results quickly — without sacrificing cultural depth or the quality of live instruction. Classes are evening-friendly, tightly focused on spoken output, and structured around high-frequency real-life situations. The signature ⚡ Speed Dating units are timed production exercises where you chain everything you've learned in a lively, pressure-free format.

Full Syllabus

10 units. Speaking from lesson one.

Each unit is a high-frequency real-life situation. The ⚡ Speed Dating units are signature timed production exercises — everything you've learned, no script, two minutes each.

01
Hello, je m'appelle…
Introduce yourself to a stranger: give your first name, country of origin, and language(s) spoken. Use je m'appelle… / je suis… / je parle…, practice the spoken alphabet, and discover French accents.
02
Bonjour, bonsoir, au revoir !
Greet appropriately at different times of day and take leave. Learn key salutation formulas for morning, afternoon, and evening, and get a first feel for the tu / vous distinction.
03
0 à 20 — les chiffres
Say your age, give a phone number, and understand a simple price. Count from 0 to 20 using j'ai X ans, and practice the nasal sounds [ɑ̃], [ɛ̃], and [ɔ̃] that shape French pronunciation.
04
C'est quoi ?
Name everyday objects and say what something is. Use c'est un… / une… / des… with 20 common nouns and develop an implicit feel for grammatical gender.
05
Les couleurs et les descriptions
Describe an object or a person using one or two adjectives. Learn colour vocabulary alongside grand / petit / beau / vieux and develop an implicit awareness of adjective agreement.
06
⚡ Speed dating — Let's recap !
Introduce yourself to three different people in two minutes each. A mid-course review of Units 1–5 with no new language: consolidate your name, country, language, age, and a description in a lively, timed format.
07
Au café
Order a drink and pay at a café. Use je voudrais… / je prends… / s'il vous plaît / merci / c'est combien ? in a short, polite exchange.
08
Les jours et les moments
Say which day and time of day you do an activity. Learn the 7 days of the week and time-of-day expressions (le matin, le midi, l'après-midi, le soir, la nuit), and use habitual forms (le lundi, le matin).
09
J'aime / je n'aime pas
Express your feelings about 5 activities or foods. Use the full opinion scale — j'adore / j'aime / je n'aime pas / je déteste — with an infinitive or a noun.
10
⚡ À bientôt !
Hold a short closing conversation: introduce yourself, share a preference, and mention a simple plan. A general review of all LSF0 language with complete politeness formulas. The final assessed task of the course.
