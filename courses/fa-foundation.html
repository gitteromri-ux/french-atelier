What you'll learn

By unit 20, you will…

Foundation takes you from silence to your first real exchanges in French — the milestones below are what A1.1 looks like in practice.

  • Greet, introduce yourself and say where you’re from with correct pronunciation
  • Order food and drink, and pay, in a Paris café or bakery
  • Count, tell the time, and handle prices, dates and phone numbers
  • Ask simple questions and understand short, slow replies
  • Master the French sounds that trip up beginners (nasals, the French R, liaison)
  • Describe your family, your home and your daily routine in the present tense

The full curriculum

Twenty units, one unforgettable first weekend

A structured A0→A1.1 curriculum, each 85-minute live unit themed to a real moment of Parisian life.

01
Arriving · First contact

Bonjour Paris

Greetings, the French alphabet and the sounds of French. Say hello, goodbye and your name with confidence.

02
Introducing yourself

Je me présente

Tell people who you are, where you’re from and what languages you speak. Tu vs. vous and the verb être.

03
At the café

Au café de Flore

Order a coffee, a croissant and water. Polite phrases, please and thank you, and reading a simple menu.

04
Numbers around town

Les chiffres de la ville

Numbers 0–69, prices in euros, and asking how much something costs at a kiosk.

05
At the bakery

À la boulangerie

Buy bread and pastries by the piece. Quantities, un/une, and the partitive in everyday shopping.

06
Telling the time

Quelle heure est-il ?

Tell and ask the time, talk about opening hours, and arrange to meet a friend.

07
My family

Ma famille

Introduce your family with possessive adjectives. Describe people simply with avoir and être.

08
In the neighbourhood

Dans le quartier

Name shops and places, and ask where things are. Il y a and basic prepositions of place.

09
Taking the metro

Prendre le métro

Buy a ticket, read a line map and ask for directions underground. Imperatives for instructions.

10
At the market

Au marché Mouffetard

Shop for fruit and vegetables, ask for quantities and react to prices. Numbers 70–100.

11
A day in Paris

Une journée à Paris

Describe your daily routine with regular -er verbs and common reflexive verbs (se lever, se coucher).

12
Likes & dislikes

J’aime, je n’aime pas

Express preferences about food, places and pastimes. Aimer, adorer, détester and negation.

13
The weekend

Le week-end

Talk about leisure: cinema, parks, museums. The verbs aller and faire, and du/de la/des.

14
At the restaurant

Au restaurant

Book a table, order a full meal and ask for the bill. Je voudrais and the conditional of politeness.

15
Going shopping

Faire les magasins

Shop for clothes, give sizes and colours, and try things on. Demonstratives ce/cette/ces.

16
Parisian weather

La météo parisienne

Talk about the weather and the seasons, and make small talk. Il fait / il y a expressions.

17
Making an appointment

Prendre rendez-vous

Phone to make a simple booking, give your details and confirm a time. Days, months and dates.

18
A postcard

Une carte postale

Write and read a short message. Basic past with the passé composé of common verbs.

19
My first story

Mon premier récit

Link sentences with et, mais, parce que to tell a short story about your day in Paris.

20
Bringing it together

Au revoir, à bientôt

Review and live role-play across the weekend’s situations. You leave at a confident A1.1.
