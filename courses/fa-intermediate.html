The full syllabus

20 lessons. One journey.

FA Intermediate spans three very different Frances in one journey: the sun and salt of Marseille — the Vieux-Port, Le Panier, the Calanques and the MuCEM — the high alpine drama of Chamonix and the Aiguille du Midi, and the storybook winter of Alsace and Champagne, from Strasbourg's Petite France to the chalk cellars of Reims.

01
Sur le Vieux-Port de Marseille

On the Vieux-Port you reactivate opinion and learn to open a debate, expressing agreement and disagreement with nuance. The can-do: state and qualify a point of view.

Marseille Vieux-Port
02
Dans les ruelles du Panier

Through Le Panier's alleyways you describe a neighbourhood and its atmosphere richly, using relative clauses with qui and que. You leave able to describe a place in connected detail.

Le Panier alleyways
03
Dans les Calanques

Among the Calanques you narrate an outdoor adventure with nuance, contrasting imparfait and passé composé. The outcome: tell a layered story of an experience.

Calanques of Provence
04
Au quartier de L'Estaque

In L'Estaque, painted by Cézanne, you describe and react to art and landscape, justifying an aesthetic opinion. You can now defend a taste with reasons.

L'Estaque
05
Au Vallon des Auffes

At the tiny harbour of the Vallon des Auffes you handle a meal with choices and dietary needs, negotiating politely. The can-do: manage a nuanced restaurant exchange.

Vallon des Auffes
06
Au MuCEM

Inside the MuCEM you compare cultures and customs, expressing similarity and difference with comparatives. You leave able to compare ways of life thoughtfully.

MuCEM
07
Face au Mont-Blanc

From a viewpoint toward Mont-Blanc you express awe and hypothesis, using si + présent for simple conditions. The outcome: talk about possibilities and reactions.

Viewpoint toward Mont-Blanc
08
Sur les glaciers de Chamonix

On the Chamonix glaciers you give and follow safety instructions and advice, using the imperative and il faut. You can now give and receive practical guidance.

Chamonix glaciers
09
Le train de la Mer de Glace

On the rack railway to the Mer de Glace you narrate a journey with sequence and feeling, handling unexpected delays. The can-do: cope with and recount a travel hiccup.

Rack railway to the Mer de Glace
10
À l'Aiguille du Midi

At 3,842 m on the Aiguille du Midi you describe sensation and reaction, expressing intensity and emotion. You leave able to convey how an extreme experience felt.

Aiguille du Midi (3,842 m)
11
Dans la Petite France de Strasbourg

In Strasbourg's Petite France you ask for and give detailed recommendations, justifying and ranking options. The outcome: advise someone and defend your advice.

Strasbourg Petite France
12
Au marché de Noël de la Place Broglie

At the Place Broglie Christmas market you negotiate a purchase, compare products, and express preference with nuance. You can now bargain and choose with confidence.

Place Broglie Christmas market
13
Devant la cathédrale de Strasbourg

Before the pink-sandstone cathedral you describe a monument and its history in connected speech, asking deeper questions. The can-do: discuss heritage at length.

Strasbourg pink-sandstone cathedral
14
Un souper au Winstub

At an Alsatian Winstub you handle a convivial meal, tell an anecdote, and react to others' stories. You leave able to take part in table conversation.

Alsatian Winstub supper
15
À la cathédrale de Reims

In Reims's royal cathedral you talk about history and tradition, expressing cause and consequence with parce que and donc. The outcome: explain why things are as they are.

Reims royal cathedral
16
Au marché couvert de Reims

In the indoor market of Reims you discuss regional produce and gastronomy, comparing and recommending specialities. You can now hold a food conversation with nuance.

Reims indoor market
17
Sur la Place d'Erlon

On the Place d'Erlon you organise an evening with a group, negotiate compromise, and resolve disagreement. The can-do: manage a group decision diplomatically.

Place d'Erlon
18
Dans les caves de Champagne

In the chalk cellars of Champagne you describe a process and tradition in detail, sequencing and explaining each stage. You leave able to talk through a complex process.

Champagne chalk cellars
19
Comparer trois Frances

You compare the three regions of your journey — Mediterranean, alpine, north-eastern — expressing nuanced contrast and personal preference. The outcome: compare cultures with depth.

MuCEM
20
Bilan — opinion, négociation, récit

Your final unit fuses opinion, negotiation and nuanced narration into one autonomous exchange — a best-of review confirming you can now produce language independently and handle the unexpected.

Champagne chalk cellars

Questions & answers

Everything you need to know

Including how it works, the certificate, and what it costs.

FA Intermediate is built for learners at CEFR A2.1 entering at A2.1 and finishing around A2.2. It assumes you've reached the level just below and want to consolidate and grow. Lessons are live, in small groups, and led by native certified teachers broadcasting from France.

You attend 20 live 85 min sessions in groups of 8–10 learners. Every unit is a real communicative task anchored in a real French location, so the language attaches to a place and a purpose. Recordings are available so you never miss a step.

Yes. On completion you receive a CEFR-aligned certificate of achievement issued by Acadomia, France's #1 private learning institute. Our framework follows the same Common European levels recognised across French academia and employers — the standard of reference used by institutions including the Sorbonne.

The French Atelier is presented by Acadomia — founded in 1989 and France's #1 private learning institute — in partnership with the eTeacher award-winning live online learning platform. You learn from native, certified French teachers, supported around the clock by Juliane, our 24/7 AI tutor.

Absolutely. An education advisor will help you place into the right course and build a plan around your schedule and goals.

FA courses run from $42 a week on the annual plan — $840 in total for all 20 live lessons — or pay monthly from $56 a week. Pricing is always discussed with your advisor so it fits your situation — there are no hidden fees, and a $100 learning credit is available on completion.
