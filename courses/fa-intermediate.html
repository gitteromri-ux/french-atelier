What you'll learn

By unit 20, you will…

Intermediate is the bridge to independence. These A2.2 milestones put B1 within reach.

  • Use the simple future and the conditional to plan, predict and advise
  • Structure an opinion with reasons, examples and counter-points
  • Narrate complex events mixing all the past tenses naturally
  • Handle the unexpected: complaints, changes of plan, problem-solving
  • Discuss culture, regions and current life with growing spontaneity
  • Understand the main ideas of clear standard speech on familiar topics
Filmed across France

Three regions, one fluent voice

Intermediate is France at its most varied — the markets and accent of Marseille, the alpine grandeur of Chamonix, and the storybook villages of Alsace. Each setting stretches your French a little further.

The full curriculum

Twenty units from the Mediterranean to the mountains

A structured A2.1→A2.2 curriculum, each 85-minute live unit set between Marseille, Chamonix and Alsace.

01
Hello Marseille

Bonjour Marseille

Re-activate A2.1 and dive into the South. Greetings, accents and talking about a city.

02
On the Old Port

Sur le Vieux-Port

Describe a place vividly and recommend it. Relative pronouns qui and que.

03
Tomorrow I will…

Demain, je ferai…

The simple future: make plans, predictions and resolutions with precision.

04
What if we left?

Si on partait ?

Introducing the conditional for suggestions, wishes and politeness.

05
Southern cuisine

La cuisine du Sud

Discuss and compare regional dishes and tastes with nuance. Pronouns combined.

06
An unexpected event

Un imprévu

Handle a change of plan or a problem. Narrate what happened and what you did.

07
Heading to Chamonix

Cap sur Chamonix

Talk about travel and landscape in the mountains. Geography and weather in detail.

08
In the mountains

À la montagne

Describe outdoor activities and give safety advice. The imperative and il faut que (intro).

09
Giving advice

Donner un conseil

Advise a friend using the conditional: tu devrais, tu pourrais, je te conseille de.

10
Recounting a memory

Raconter un souvenir

Tell a layered story mixing passé composé, imparfait and plus-que-parfait (intro).

11
Mountain seasons

Les saisons en montagne

Compare seasons and describe change over time. Expressions of duration and frequency.

12
Debate at the table

Débat à table

Express and defend an opinion in a group. Agreeing, disagreeing and nuance.

13
Towards Alsace

Direction l’Alsace

Plan and describe a regional trip. Future and conditional side by side.

14
The wine route

Sur la route des vins

Discuss traditions, festivals and crafts. Vocabulary of heritage and savoir-faire.

15
In Strasbourg

À Strasbourg

Navigate a bilingual, European city. Talk about identity, borders and belonging.

16
Expressing a wish

Exprimer un souhait

Talk about dreams and hopes for the future. Conditional and j’aimerais que (intro).

17
Writing a formal letter

Écrire une lettre formelle

Write a clear formal request or complaint. Register, structure and politeness formulas.

18
Comparing lifestyles

Comparer les modes de vie

Compare city, coast and mountain life. Superlatives and balanced argument.

19
The grand debate

Le grand débat

A live structured debate on a cultural theme. Opinion, reasons and rebuttal.

20
Towards B1

Vers le B1

A capstone role-play across all three regions. You finish a confident A2.2, ready for B1.
