Course Overview

Speaking French with real autonomy

For graduates of LSF Beginner. LSF Elementary addresses the more complex situations that arise when settling into life in France: sharing opinions about French culture, handling problems and complaints, making appointments by phone, booking outings, talking about work and projects, narrating past events, managing administrative tasks, and decoding French cultural implicits. By the end of this module, you can produce language with real autonomy and adapt your register to the situation.

LSF Elementary is the top of the LSF track. It demands and builds the kind of cultural fluency that only comes from sustained live practice with native speakers. The final ⚡ Speed Dating unit — Ma vie en France — asks you to sustain a 2-minute free exchange across 5 random situations, adapting register from formal to informal as required. Nothing is scripted. Everything is real.

Full Syllabus

10 units. From opinions to cultural fluency.

Complaints, appointments, past narration, admin, cultural implicits — and a final 2-minute free production test that proves you've arrived.

01
Et alors, tu aimes la France ?
Narrate your arrival in France in a 4–5 sentence oral account, using at least one verb in the passé composé. Express an opinion with je trouve que… and react to your interlocutor's view. Discover what international residents find surprising, charming, or baffling about French life.
02
Il y a un problème…
Signal a problem clearly (excusez-moi, il y a un problème avec…), make a polite request using est-ce que vous pouvez [demande] ?, and hold your ground if the first response is a refusal. Practice handling at least 2 unexpected twists without losing composure.
03
Prendre rendez-vous
Make an appointment by phone: introduce yourself, state the purpose of your call, and negotiate an alternative slot if the first is unavailable. Use est-ce que vous avez une disponibilité [jour/heure] ? and confirm politely. Full simulation without visual support.
04
Sortir à Paris
Propose an outing with a day and venue (tu veux [activité] avec moi [jour] ?), reserve tickets (je voudrais réserver [nombre] places pour [spectacle]), and accept or decline with a reason. Agree on a meeting point and time. Discover the cultural offer of French cities.
05
Mon travail, mes projets
Give a 5-sentence semi-formal professional introduction: name your job and sector, describe your work environment, and talk about a near-future project using je vais + infinitif. Answer 2 follow-up questions without a script.
06
⚡ Speed dating : La vie à Paris
Chain the 5 speech acts from Units 1–5 in a speed dating format, with one unexpected prompt per situation and no written support. Consolidation of LSF2 Units 1–5, with recycling of key language from LSF1.
07
Je t'explique…
Narrate a recent event in 4–5 sentences using the passé composé with avoir (regular verbs + fait, vu, passé). Sequence with d'abord… et ensuite…, reactivate at least one speech act from a previous unit, and respond to one off-script question from your interlocutor.
08
Vie pratique
Handle a simple administrative task at a bank or post office: state your request in formal register (je voudrais ouvrir un compte / récupérer un colis), understand the procedure, and write a short 2–3 sentence complaint message. Discover the practical realities of administrative life in France.
09
Comprendre les Français
Decode two French cultural situations, identify an implicit refusal, and react appropriately. Use clarification strategies (je ne suis pas sûr(e) de comprendre… tu veux dire que… ?) and compare with your own cultural background in 2–3 sentences.
10
⚡ Speed dating : Ma vie en France
Final consolidation of all LSF2 speech acts in entirely free production. Five situations drawn at random from Units 1–9, each with unexpected prompts, adapting register (formal / informal) as required, with a minimum total exchange of 2 minutes. The final assessed task of the course.
