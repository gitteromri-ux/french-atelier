What is a capsule?

One icon, one hour, one bilingual storyteller — every week for ten weeks.

i.

One story, beautifully told

Each conference takes a single cultural icon — a king, a couturière, a cheese, a chanson — and tells its story live, with the images, music and anecdotes that made it part of France.

ii.

One hour, then your questions

A live one-hour conference on Zoom, followed by fifteen to twenty minutes of open questions with the speaker. Once a week, ten weeks per pack.

iii.

A bilingual signature speaker

Delivered in English by a bilingual expert who lives the subject, with the French words culture actually uses — terroir, silhouette, polar — explained as they appear. No French required.

iv.

Three packs, thirty conferences

Fashion & Art, Gastronomy & Wine, Cinema & Music. Take one pack, two, or all three — or pair them with a French Atelier course in the Atelier Membership.
