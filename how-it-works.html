Five Steps

Your complete Atelier journey

01
Step One · Before You Begin

Assessment & Enrollment

Choose your track — FA1 Beginner or FA2 Advanced — after a live placement call with a French Atelier advisor. This is not a written test; it is a conversation. Our advisors listen for where you are in your French life, and place you in the course where you will thrive, not merely survive.

Whether you are arriving at French for the very first time or returning after years away, the placement call ensures that your first live class is a moment of confidence — not confusion. The right track from the start is the foundation of everything that follows.

Live placement call FA Foundation / Beginner / Elementary / Intermediate tracks Let's Speak French (LSF) evening track Advisor-guided enrollment
02
Step Two · Your Digital Home

Join the Learning Platform

Access your dashboard, schedule, course materials, and community — all in one elegant, purpose-built learning environment. No downloads. No complexity. Just a laptop and a microphone, and France opens to you.

Your platform is the Atelier beyond the live class — where recordings live, where study materials await, where the community gathers between sessions, and where Juliane, your 24/7 AI French coach, is always on call. From the moment you log in for the first time, you are inside the French Atelier world.

Personal dashboard Class schedule & calendar Course materials & cultural resources Community forum Juliane AI tutor access
03
Step Three · The Heart of It

Attend Weekly Live Classes

85-minute live lessons with native certified teachers, broadcast from iconic locations in France. Small groups of 8–10 learners ensure that every class is a genuine conversation — not a lecture.

Classes run Sunday through Friday, with times calibrated across timezones so that wherever you are in the world, France is always accessible. Each lesson begins in a real cultural place — a market, a gallery, a village square — and uses that context to teach the French of lived experience. You will speak more in one French Atelier class than in weeks of conventional study.

85 minutes per session 8–10 learners per group Native certified teachers Broadcast live from France Sunday – Friday scheduling Timezone-friendly times

04
Step Four · Deepening the Learning

Practice & Reinforcement

Each lesson is recorded and available on-demand inside your platform. Between live classes, complete structured micro-tasks and join a weekly study group to consolidate what you have learned and keep your momentum alive.

The study group — a twice-weekly peer session available on annual plans — is where ideas from the live class are rehearsed, debated, and made permanent. Juliane bridges the gaps: available at any hour for pronunciation drills, vocabulary practice, cultural questions, and spontaneous conversation. The French Atelier experience does not end when the live class closes. It continues until the next one begins.

All classes recorded On-demand replay Structured micro-tasks Weekly study groups (annual plan) Juliane AI · 24/7
05
Step Five · Your Achievement

Mastery & Certification

Complete 20 lessons to earn a CEFR-aligned certificate from ACADOMIA — and receive a $100 cashback credit toward your next course. This is not a participation ribbon. It is a verified mark of communicative attainment, internationally recognised.

The French Atelier certificate, issued by Acadomia — France's leading private learning institute — carries the weight of a 35-year institution behind it. It marks a genuine milestone: a new relationship with the French language, with French culture, and with the world that French unlocks. Many learners continue immediately into the next course. France, once entered, is difficult to leave.

20 lessons to completion CEFR-aligned certificate Issued by Acadomia $100 cashback credit Pathway to next level

Community & Connection

The French Atelier is a living community of learners across the globe — connected by a shared love of France, supported by the platform, the forum, and one another. Language acquisition is a social act.

What Supports the Journey

Three pillars, always present

◈

Live Lessons with World-Class Experts

Every class is led by a native, certified French teacher broadcasting live from their region of France. These are not tutors. They are cultural guides, language artists, and dedicated educators who make every 85 minutes extraordinary.

◈

Online Forum Support

The French Atelier community forum connects learners across the globe — a space to practise, to ask, to celebrate, and to find the companions who make language learning a shared adventure rather than a solitary task.

Wealth of Academic & Cultural Content

Beyond the live class, an ever-growing library of cultural resources — articles, readings, audio, and video — deepens your relationship with the French world. Language and culture are, at the Atelier, inseparable.

Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

All classes are live — never pre-recorded. You learn in real time with a native, certified teacher broadcasting live from France.

How big are the groups?

Small Zoom groups of 8–10 learners for maximum speaking time and personal attention.

What do I need to join?

Just a laptop and a microphone. No downloads or special software required.

Can I watch classes I missed?

Yes. Every live class is recorded and available on-demand inside your learning platform — with lifetime access.
