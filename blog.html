Cultural Journal

From Our Editors

Where language and culture meet

The Cultural Journal is our literary companion to learning French — a space where the language breathes. Each essay is an invitation: to the market on a Tuesday morning, to the cinema queue on a rainy Wednesday, to the table d'hôte of a Burgundy auberge. Read them as you would a letter from France. Let the words arrive slowly.
