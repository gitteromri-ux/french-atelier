1. Contact us

  • Email (enrolled students, all requests): service@eteachergroup.com
  • WhatsApp (chat): WhatsApp Customer Service: +1 (202) 949-4750
  • Admissions (before you enroll): advisor@eTeacherGroup.com · Contact form
  • Response time: within one business day. Every message is read and answered by a member of the eTeacher Group Customer Care team.
  • Privacy requests: Privacy@eteachergroup.com (see our Privacy Policy).

When you write to us, include the email address you registered with and, if relevant, your order number, your course or capsule, and the lesson date. That lets us answer in one reply.

2. Live lessons and Culture Capsules

Zoom access

Lessons take place live on Zoom in small groups. Your Zoom link and calendar invitation are sent to your registered email after enrollment and again before each lesson. If you cannot find them, email or WhatsApp us at least two hours before the lesson starts and we will resend them.

Recordings

Every live lesson is recorded and made available on demand inside your learning platform. Recordings are for enrolled students only and are not to be shared or reposted.

Changing your group, level or schedule

To move to another group or time, change your level after a placement conversation, or update your details, email us with your current enrollment and the change you want. We confirm every change in writing.

Certificate

On completion of the 20-lesson course you receive a CEFR-aligned A1 or A2 certificate issued by Acadomia. If your certificate has not arrived, email us with your full name and course dates.

Technical requirements

A laptop or tablet, a microphone and a stable internet connection. Zoom is free; no other software is required. If you have a problem joining, tell us the device, operating system and what you see on screen. A screenshot helps.

3. Payments and receipts

Payments are processed in US Dollars. A receipt is emailed automatically after each payment. If you pay in instalments, each payment is confirmed by email. If you need an invoice in another name or format, email service@eteachergroup.com.

4. Cancellations and refunds

Culture Capsules: 14-day full refund. Capsule enrollments are fully refundable within 14 days from the registration date.

For the French course, refund eligibility is determined by the plan selected at the time of enrollment, as communicated during the enrollment process and set out in our Terms & Conditions. If your circumstances change, contact us as early as possible.

To cancel, email service@eteachergroup.com from your registered address with your full name and the course or capsule you selected. Where a refund is granted it is returned to the original payment method; allow 5 to 10 business days for your bank or card issuer to show it.

5. Your account and data

To correct your details, or to delete your account and the personal data associated with it, email service@eteachergroup.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm the request, close the account and delete your personal data, except where we are required by law to keep records (for example invoices). Your rights and how we handle them are described in our Privacy Policy; privacy-specific requests can also go to Privacy@eteachergroup.com.

6. Accessibility

If any part of our website or lessons is difficult for you to use, tell us and we will provide the material in an alternative format where we can.
