How It Works

Five steps from first bonjour to certification

From your first live enrollment call to your CEFR certificate — every step supported by our advanced, world-class learning platform.

01

Assessment & Enrollment

Select French Atelier 1 (Beginner) or French Atelier 2 (Advanced), share your details and preferred schedule, and enroll instantly on a live call with one of our service professionals.

02

Join Our Learning Platform

Receive instant access to our advanced, world-class learning platform — with all the information and full visibility on your upcoming course in one elegant dashboard.

03

Attend Your Live Online Classes

Join weekly 85-minute online classes led by native French teachers, live from France — culture-based learning broadcast from real French cultural hotspots.

04

Practice & Reinforcement

After each class your portal unlocks the recording and auto-checked micro-tasks — then book an additional weekly study-group session with guided conversations and Q&A to cement progress.

05

Mastery & Certification

Complete all 20 lessons and receive a French Atelier certificate aligned to your new CEFR level — and claim $100 cash-back credits toward your next course.
