The French Atelier by Acadomia

A language is not a subject.
It is a way of living.

French Atelier by Acadomia is an exclusive, culturally immersive, certified, live French language program. We don't teach grammar in a vacuum — we teach French where it is lived: at the café counter, in the market queue, on the quays of the Seine. Every lesson begins in a real place and ends in a real conversation.

Our Method

Pedagogy built on Sorbonne research

Our curriculum and methodology are grounded in research from the Sorbonne in applied linguistics and second-language acquisition. Every unit is designed around a real communicative task — not a grammar rule — so you acquire French the way it is actually spoken.

Live lessons with world-class experts · Online forum support · A wealth of academic and cultural content — all built around the way the brain truly learns a language.
