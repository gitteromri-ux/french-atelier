The Science of Our Method

Built on the action-oriented tradition of the Sorbonne

The French Atelier method is built upon the modern science of language acquisition — the action-oriented, communicative tradition advanced by applied linguists at the Sorbonne and codified in the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Live French. Live. — because learners don't merely learn about the language, they live it.

l'approche actionnelle

You act, you speak

You are a social agent who acts with intention — ordering, asking, negotiating, connecting — not memorising rules. Every lesson moves you toward doing something real in French.

situated learning

Learned where it's lived

Every unit is anchored to a real French place and situation. Small live groups of 8–10 are built for interaction and mediation — not lectures.

culture au cœur

Culture at the core

Certified to the CEFR (A0 → A2.2) and aligned to DELF/DALF “can-do” assessment — so cultural fluency and measurable progress advance together.

Grounded in peer-reviewed applied-linguistics research

Piccardo & North (2019) · The Action-Oriented Approach Council of Europe (2020) · CEFR Companion Volume Sorbonne Nouvelle · Applied Linguistics / Didactique des langues CASLT · Action-Oriented Approach

Our method draws on the Sorbonne tradition of applied linguistics and the CEFR. The French Atelier is independent and not affiliated with, nor endorsed by, the Sorbonne.
