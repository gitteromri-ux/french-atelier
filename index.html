A Research-Grade Curriculum

Designed Curriculum, based upon theSorbonne linguistics research & methodologies.

Action-oriented, communicative learning — grounded in the applied-linguistics tradition of the Sorbonne and the Common European Framework. You don’t study French. You act in it.

01 l’approche actionnelle

You act, you speak

A social agent acts with intention — ordering, asking, negotiating. Every lesson moves you toward doing something real in French.

02 situated learning

Learned where it’s lived

Every unit is anchored to a real French place. Small live groups of 8–10 built for interaction — not lectures.

03 culture au cœur

Culture at the core

CEFR-aligned (A0 → A2.2) with DELF/DALF can-do assessment — cultural fluency and measurable progress, together.
