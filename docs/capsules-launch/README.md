## 2. Media plan — first 10 days

**[→ media-plan.md](./media-plan.md)**

Executive summary:

1. **$500/day → $5,000 over 10 days** (Thu 15 Oct – Sat 24 Oct 2026). Purchase optimization, 7d click / 1d view, lowest cost.
2. **3 ad sets:** 01 Prospecting-Interests $200 · 02 Prospecting-Lookalikes (all past FA purchasers) $200 · 03 Retargeting (purchasers, 90-day visitors, checkout abandoners, form starters, video viewers) $100.
3. **15 creatives at launch:** 3 hero videos, 4 UGC, 8 banners / stories / reels. 5 ads per ad set (1–2 hero videos, 1 short UGC, 2 reels/banners/stories) to exit learning fast.
4. **Day-by-day table:** spend per set, CPM, CPC, link clicks, landing page views, checkouts, LPV→checkout, checkout→purchase, LPV→purchase, purchases, CPA, revenue, ROAS.
5. **Target by Day 10:** 5–10 purchases (model: 8 at ~$620 CPA, ROAS 0.69). Not profitable yet by design; this is the signal test.
6. **Gates:** Day 3 delivery · Day 5 intent · Day 7 trend · Day 10 verdict. Scale path if green: +20% per 48 h, never more.
