Executive summary:

1. **$500/day → $5,000 over 10 days** (Wed 21 Oct – Fri 30 Oct 2026). Sales objective, Purchase optimization, 7d click / 1d view, lowest cost, ad-set budgets.
2. **3 ad sets:** 01 Prospecting-Interests $200 · 02 Lookalikes of all-time French Atelier purchasers $200 · 03 Retargeting (all FA purchasers, 90-day visitors, checkout abandoners, form starters, engaged page actions) $100.
3. **15 creatives at launch:** 3 hero videos, 4 UGC, 8 banners / stories / reels. 5 ads per ad set (1–2 hero videos, 1 short UGC, 2 reels/banners/stories) to exit learning fast.
4. **Benchmarks:** CPM $47.8 / CPC $3.25 / link CTR 1.47% — VERIFIED from the French Atelier v2 ad account (act_1358975119724184), last 30 days, Meta API, 19 Oct 2026. Funnel rates below the click are ASSUMED and labelled in the plan.
5. **Day-by-day table** (Julie CEO-plan style): spend per set, cum spend, CPM, impressions, CPC, link clicks, LPV, checkouts, LPV→checkout, checkout→purchase, LPV→purchase, purchases, CPA, revenue, ROAS. Plus a per-ad-set summary.
6. **Model by Day 10:** 8.2 purchases at ~$606 CPA, ROAS 0.7, blended CPM $40.8, CPC $2.6, LPV→purchase 0.6%. Not profitable yet by design; this is the signal test.
7. **Gates:** Day 3 delivery (Fri 23 Oct) · Day 5 intent (Sun 25 Oct) · Day 7 trend (Tue 27 Oct) · Day 10 verdict (Fri 30 Oct), explicit thresholds in the plan. Scale path if green: +20% per 48 h, never more.
