Who They Are

Teachers who are the culture they teach

The French Atelier teachers are not instructors who happen to speak French. They are people for whom French is the texture of daily life — the language of their market, their kitchen, their friendships, their city. When they teach, they do not describe France. They bring it.

Each teacher is native, certified, and deeply embedded in a specific region of France — so that across the course of a year, learners encounter not one France but many: the formal grandeur of Paris, the maritime warmth of Normandy, the sun-drenched energy of Marseille, the Alsatian particularity of Strasbourg. To learn French at The French Atelier is to learn it in all its glorious geographic variety.

The Team

Eight teachers. Eight regions. One France.

Strasbourg · Alsace
Caitlin
Strasbourg

Rooted at the crossroads of France and Germany, Caitlin teaches contextual French with the precision and warmth of Alsatian culture — where every word carries the weight of a rich bilingual history and the pride of one of France's most distinctive regions.

Paris · Île-de-France
Carmèle
Paris

A Parisienne by birth and by sensibility, Carmèle brings the capital's intellectual rigour and social grace into every class — teaching the French of the gallery, the salon, and the café terrace with the easy authority of someone who has lived it all her life.

Paris · Île-de-France
Charline
Paris

Charline's approach to teaching French is lit by a deep love of Parisian literature and contemporary culture — she guides learners through the language of the city with the curiosity of a writer and the generosity of a guide who wants you to fall in love with what she loves.

Pau · Pyrénées-Atlantiques
Corentin
Pau

From the foothills of the Pyrénées, Corentin brings the unhurried, melodious French of the South-West — a region where Gascon pride, Basque spirit, and Béarnaise warmth meet in a language that flows like mountain water, rich and clear and entirely its own.

Lyon · Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes
Iris Linza
Lyon

Lyon — France's gastronomic capital and cultural powerhouse — is the source of Iris's teaching: she weaves the language of the bouchon, the traboule, and the city's renowned silk-weaving heritage into lessons that are as nourishing as the city itself.

West Paris · Île-de-France
Philippe
West Paris

Philippe teaches with the quiet authority of a man who has spent a lifetime in the western quartiers of Paris — where culture, film, and the art of living converge — bringing his learners into the conversational French of the city's most storied neighbourhoods with ease and elegance.

Montpellier · Occitanie
Shanice
Montpellier

Shanice teaches the warm, sun-drenched French of the Mediterranean coast — where Occitan inflections colour the vowels, where the rhythm of life is generous and unhurried, and where the language opens like a window onto a France that is vivid, sensory, and utterly alive.

Nice · Côte d'Azur
Stan
Nice

From the shimmering coast of the Côte d'Azur, Stan brings the luminous, cosmopolitan French of Nice — a city that has been home to artists, aristocrats, and adventurers for two centuries — teaching a French that sparkles with the light of the Mediterranean and the sophistication of its storied waterfront.

The Atelier Difference

Certified, native, and broadcasting live

Every French Atelier teacher is native — not near-native, not heritage, but living and breathing French in France, every day of their lives. And every class they teach is live: a real-time, unscripted communicative event that cannot be faked or pre-recorded.

Beyond native fluency, every teacher is professionally certified — trained in communicative language teaching, in second-language acquisition, and in the specific culture-first methodology that defines the French Atelier approach. They are not only speakers; they are educators, with the craft to bring a language to life for someone who is hearing its music for the first time.
